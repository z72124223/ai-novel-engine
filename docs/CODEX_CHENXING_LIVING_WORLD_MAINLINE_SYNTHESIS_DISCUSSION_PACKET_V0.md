## Preserved A04 Synthesis Sections

### Living-World Foundation

The mainline should be discussed as a living-world foundation first. Character,
profession, class, city, wilderness, threat, and product-chain choices should
serve a world that feels active before it serves a pipeline artifact.

### 陸知行 Curiosity / Logic / Clue-Assembly Axis

陸知行's planning axis is curiosity, logic, clue assembly, and learning the
world. Discussion should preserve observation, comparison, problem-solving, and
learning pressure before deciding any mainline arc.

### 愛醬 Independent Growth / Anti-Toolification Axis

愛醬's growth should remain independent. She may have unseen-side developments,
reactions, sensory attention, and relationship beats. She must not be reduced
to pet, tool, clue machine, mascot, combat asset, or mount-only support.

### Outside-City Monster / Exhaustion / Dragon Pressure

Outside-city pressure should remain a live discussion axis: monster pressure,
exhaustion, travel cost, ecological danger, vertical danger, and dragon-scale
threat must shape what the world asks of characters.

### Inside-City Identity / Class / Profession Pressure

Inside-city pressure should remain a live discussion axis: identity systems,
class access, profession dignity, registration, practical survival, and social
gatekeeping should define what the city asks of characters.

### Profession Ecology

Profession ecology should keep cook, apothecary, and doctor distinctions open:

- cook: survival, restoration, morale, difficult monster-ingredient handling,
  food safety, and social access;
- apothecary: material recognition, preparation, dosage, trade trust, and
  boundary between remedy and poison;
- doctor: diagnosis, injury care, body knowledge, crisis handling, and ethical
  treatment boundaries.

No profession should be dismissed as useless. The cook example is especially a
profession-dignity guard, not a joke role and not a disposable support label.
