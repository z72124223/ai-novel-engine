## 6. New World Foundation Requirements

A replacement world bible should be built before new generation or asset pickup resumes.

Minimum required layers:

### Core Premise

- What kind of world is this?
- What is the emotional and thematic promise?
- Why does the protagonist's arrival matter now?
- What is the first concrete pressure before the local main plot takes over?

### Era / Material Culture

- historical / fantasy / cultivation / otherworld tech level;
- allowed materials;
- forbidden modern artifacts;
- transport and road assumptions;
- medicine, weapons, lighting, clothing, documents, and storage constraints;
- exception sources such as transported objects, relics, foreign goods, or magic.

### Geography

- macro region;
- first arrival place;
- nearby safe / unsafe zones;
- travel friction;
- weather / terrain / visibility;
- how the opening location connects to later social spaces.

### Social Structure

- social ranks and local authority;
- family / clan / sect / village / market relations;
- outsider treatment;
- gender / age / profession expectations if relevant;
- who can help, block, question, or exploit the protagonist.

### Power / Cultivation / Skill System

- whether cultivation, martial arts, skills, spirits, tools, or contracts exist;
- what ordinary people can do;
- what the protagonist cannot do yet;
- what Ai-chan can sense but not explain;
- cost, training, risk, and visibility of power.

### Economy / Resource Pressure

- what people need;
- scarcity and trade;
- food, shelter, medicine, money, tools, road access;
- what immediate survival pressure can pull the protagonist into local systems.

### Law / Institution

- who enforces rules;
- what counts as evidence;
- how strangers are registered or distrusted;
- what procedures can block the protagonist without feeling arbitrary.

### Initial Scene Set

- a small approved starting scene set;
- zones / blocking areas;
- transition conditions;
- public visual descriptions;
- what must not be revealed early.

### NPC Tiering

- Tier 1: opening-critical NPCs with goals, pressure, knowledge boundary, and relationship potential.
- Tier 2: local pressure / procedure NPCs.
- Tier 3: background crowd or atmosphere.
- No NPC should exist only to recite setting.

### Protagonist Displacement Model

- how the protagonist enters;
- what evidence tells them something is wrong;
- why immediate goals are orientation / safety / return path;
- how the local plot becomes unavoidable only after failed orientation, blocked return, need for help, or companion risk.

### Ai-chan Sensory / Companion Role

- what Ai-chan can smell / hear / notice;
- how Ai-chan's autonomy creates pressure;
- how recall works;
- when Ai-chan's senses are useful, ambiguous, or misleading;
- how to avoid turning Ai-chan into a clue dispenser.

### Asset Strategy

- whether this world needs production art;
- first stable scene references;
- protagonist and Ai-chan reference needs;
- what must not be sent to 07 / 08 until foundation approval;
- which concepts are text-only until reviewed.

### Author-Only Boundary

- hidden truth categories;
- secrets not available to characters;
- information that cannot appear in prompts, user-facing packets, or asset descriptions;
- distinction between public premise and author-only design.

- named cultivation systems or exclusive power rules;
- recognizable plot arcs or scene sequences;
- signature prose style or catchphrases;

The new world must become original before it becomes a setting pack or production pipeline.
