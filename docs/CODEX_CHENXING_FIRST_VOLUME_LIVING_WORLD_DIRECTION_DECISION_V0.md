## Worldview Guards

- Current-facing protagonist name is only `陸知行`.
- `衛昭` is superseded in derived/current-facing materials and must not be
  used as the current-facing protagonist name.
- 愛醬 has her own independent story line and must not be reduced to pet, tool,
  clue machine, mascot, combat asset, or mount-only growth.
- living world first: final-product chains serve the world later.
- Old opening/mainline/Rain Gate/Pilot/demo material is historical discussion
  evidence only, not locked plot, not canon, and not final-product content.
- Profession dignity is required. No profession is useless by default.
- If cook, apothecary, or doctor appears in later planning, their distinctions
  must remain intact:
  - cook: survival, restoration, morale, food safety, difficult
    monster-ingredient handling, and social access;
  - apothecary: material recognition, preparation, dosage, trade trust, and the
    boundary between remedy and poison;
  - doctor: diagnosis, injury care, body knowledge, crisis handling, and
    ethical treatment boundaries.
