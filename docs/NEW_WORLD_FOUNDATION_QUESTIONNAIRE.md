# New World Foundation Questionnaire

Status: `DOCS_ONLY_USER_INPUT_QUESTIONNAIRE`

Date: 2026-06-22

This questionnaire is an input surface for the user / 00 to answer before 01 drafts a world foundation brief. It is not canon, not a setting pack, not runtime data, not a 04 scene packet, not a storyboard pickup brief, and not a production asset plan.

## 1. Scope And Status

Use this document to collect decisions for the replacement world foundation.

Current boundaries:

- Qingli herb-warehouse material is historical sample / process evidence only.
- Qingli must not be used as the new opening base or assetization starting point.
- The current final deliverable target is reviewable storyboards, meaning storyboard images / storyboard packets.
- High-quality animation, Seedance, Oiioii, or video generation is optional future downstream work only.
- This questionnaire must not be treated as user approval to resume 04 / 05 / 06 / 07 / 08.
- This questionnaire must not become a formal setting pack, runtime schema, Validator rule, StateReducer behavior, or SQLite data.

Answer style:

- Short answers are acceptable.
- "Unknown / let 01 propose" is acceptable.
- If an answer is only a mood or inspiration, mark it as direction rather than canon.
- Do not copy protected characters, sects, places, proprietary power systems, plot arcs, or identifiable prose style from copyrighted works.

## 2. Preserved Seeds

### 2.1 Protagonist

Questions:

- What public protagonist traits must be preserved?
- What should remain unknown for now?
- What is the protagonist's prior-world baseline: ordinary person, trained person, scholar, craftsperson, worker, traveler, student, or other?
- What emotional baseline should the opening start from: anxious, practical, stubborn, curious, exhausted, protective, numb, or other?
- What should the protagonist know at the moment of arrival?
- What should the protagonist definitely not know yet?
- What is the protagonist's first natural objective: orientation, safety, return path, shelter, food, help, companion protection, or something else?

Do not answer by inventing hidden destiny, secret power, faction lineage, reincarnation rule, or author-only truth unless the user explicitly wants that to be explored later.

### 2.2 Ai-chan

Known preserved direction from prior decisions:

- long-haired husky / snow-dog candidate;
- usually no leash;
- follows through companionship habit and a small number of commands;
- highly sensitive senses;
- autonomous and curious;
- recallable by the protagonist;
- not humanized into a person in animal shape;
- sensory writing should use visible behavior such as nose, ears, paws, body angle, stopping, turning back, and scent direction.

Questions:

- Which of the above are fixed?
- Which are flexible?
- Is Ai-chan a normal dog, an unusually perceptive dog, or a setting-touched animal?
- What commands or cues does Ai-chan respond to?
- How far can Ai-chan wander before the protagonist can call her back?
- What scents / sounds / visual cues should Ai-chan be especially good at noticing?
- What should Ai-chan not be able to solve?
- Does Ai-chan have a visual reference direction: coat length, color, size, gait, eyes, markings?

### 2.3 Relationship And Carried Items

Questions:

- How long have the protagonist and Ai-chan been together?
- Is the relationship family-like, companion-like, working-dog-like, rescue-dog-like, or another texture?
- What does Ai-chan trust the protagonist to do?
- What does the protagonist fear might happen to Ai-chan in the new world?
- What modern items, if any, arrive with the protagonist?
- Are phone, watch, keys, wallet, medicine, leash, flashlight, bag, food, or water present?
- Are those items usable, damaged, out of battery, culturally dangerous, or hidden?
- Should modern items become a story problem, a survival resource, or mostly unavailable?

## 3. Core World Premise

Questions:

- At first glance, what kind of world is this?
- Is the core texture survival, cultivation, martial world, folk horror, law-and-market pressure, wandering road story, sect frontier, village mystery, borderland, exile, disaster aftermath, or something else?
- What should the world feel like before it is explained?
- What is the first thing the protagonist should fear?
- What is the first thing the protagonist should want to understand?
- What is the first thing the protagonist should protect?
- What local pressure can pull a stranger into the world without feeling like plot gravity?
- What should the world promise long term: becoming stronger, surviving institutions, understanding local rules through contact and evidence, finding a way home, protecting Ai-chan, earning belonging, or another arc?

Avoid using an existing work's named places, factions, cultivation ladder, sect structure, signature rules, or plot beats.

## 4. Era And Material Culture

Questions:

- What is the baseline tech level?
- What materials are common: wood, bamboo, iron, bronze, cloth, leather, paper, oil, stone, clay, bone, lacquer, glass, jade, other?
- What materials are rare or forbidden: rubber, plastic, modern steel alloys, concrete, electric parts, firearms, petroleum products, synthetic fabric, modern medicine?
- How do people travel: walking, cart, horse, mule, boat, sedan chair, caravan, flying tool, spirit beast, other?
- How do people light spaces: oil lamp, candle, torch, moonlight, talisman, spirit light, electricity, other?
- How do people write and store records: bamboo slips, paper, ledger, carved token, seal, oral witness, magic record, other?
- What money or exchange medium exists?
- What weapons are normal for ordinary people?
- What weapons or tools imply institutions or supply chains that need explanation?
- What modern words or objects should be translated into period-appropriate equivalents?
- If the player brings a modern item, is it treated as transported residue, forbidden anomaly, useless object, or valuable problem?

Review check:

- Does any object imply a supply chain the world has not authorized?
- If an item like a tire, plastic container, flashlight, phone, car, firearm, or electric lamp appears, what is its source?

## 5. Power / Cultivation / Skill System

Questions:

- Does the world have cultivation, martial arts, spirits, contracts, techniques, medicine, tools, rituals, bloodline power, or no supernatural system?
- Can ordinary people perceive the power system?
- Can ordinary people practice it?
- What is the first visible sign of power?
- What is the cost of using power: time, injury, hunger, materials, debt, attention, social risk, corruption, other?
- What resources gate advancement?
- Who controls training, texts, teachers, medicine, tools, or permission?
- What can the protagonist not do at the beginning?
- What can Ai-chan sense without explaining?
- How should power be visualized in storyboards without overusing glow / generic magic effects?
- What terms should be avoided because they belong too closely to protected inspiration sources?

Answer as original principles, not as copied ranks, sect names, named techniques, or proprietary systems.
